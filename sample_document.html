GreenLeaf Solar — Company Overview & 2025 Report

GreenLeaf Solar is a renewable energy company founded in 2014 in Galway, Ireland, by engineer Maria O'Connell. The company designs and installs residential and commercial solar panel systems across Europe. As of 2025, GreenLeaf employs 480 people and operates in seven countries: Ireland, the United Kingdom, France, Spain, Germany, the Netherlands, and Portugal.

Mission

GreenLeaf's mission is to make clean solar energy affordable for every household in Europe by 2035. The company reinvests 15% of annual profit into research on battery storage technology.

Revenue grew by approximately 45% year over year, driven largely by strong demand in Germany and Spain, which together accounted for 38% of all new installations in 2025.

Flagship Product

The company's best-selling product is the GreenLeaf SunMax 400, a high-efficiency solar panel with a rated output of 400 watts and a 25-year warranty. The SunMax 400 converts up to 22.5% of sunlight into electricity, which is above the industry average of 20%.

Sustainability Goals

  • Become fully carbon-neutral in all operations by 2030.
  • Recycle 95% of decommissioned panels through a take-back programme.
  • Plant one tree for every installation completed (over 13,000 trees in 2025).

Leadership

Maria O'Connell serves as Chief Executive Officer. The Chief Technology Officer is David Kumar, who joined in 2018 and leads the battery storage research division. The company's headquarters remain in Galway, with regional offices in Berlin and Madrid.

Challenges

The main challenges identified in the 2025 report are supply-chain delays for semiconductor components and a shortage of certified installation technicians. To address the skills gap, GreenLeaf launched a training academy in 2025 that has so far certified 320 new technicians.
